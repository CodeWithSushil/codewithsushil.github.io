About me

I’m a passionate self-taught developer and open-source contributor from Odisha, India. I enjoy writing code, solving complex problems, and building developer tools and solutions for the PHP ecosystem. My work focuses on creating practical, developer-friendly software and contributing to open source. Some of my notable projects include Supabase-PHP, which has surpassed 2,000 downloads, and JSON-DB, a zero-configuration database designed for small blogs, articles, and lightweight applications. Alongside development and open source, I create programming content on YouTube, where I share knowledge, tutorials, and practical insights with fellow developers.
